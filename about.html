About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Research Scholar in the Department of Applied Physics at Stanford University. I earned my Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering (Biophotonics concentration) from the University of California, Irvine in December 2025, where I worked in the Digman Lab, and my B.S. in Biomedical Engineering from Penn State.

I have over five years of research experience designing and integrating advanced optical instrumentation for high-precision photonic measurement and imaging. I developed PRISMOS (Phasor-Resolved Hyperspectral Imaging with Single Excitation), a phasor-based hyperspectral imaging methodology (patent pending) for multiplexed organelle phenotyping in chemoresistant triple-negative breast cancer cells.

My work bridges optics, computational signal processing, and deep learning: multi-wavelength laser systems, fluorescence lifetime and hyperspectral detection, phasor-based spectral unmixing, and physics-informed neural networks for extracting quantitative information from high-dimensional optical data.

Research Highlights

  • Developed PRISMOS, integrating confocal, two-photon, and FLIM-based hyperspectral imaging with phasor unmixing models to visualize subcellular dynamics at high spatial resolution.
  • Collaborated with physics and AI researchers on stochastic, physics-informed neural networks for live-cell imaging data compression and prediction.
  • Contributed to publications in Nature Methods (ESPRESSO, in press), Biochemistry, and ACS Photonics.
  • Optical Engineer Intern at KLA, performing tolerancing analysis and design trade-off evaluation for optical systems.

Honors

Teaching & Community

I am passionate about mentorship and science communication. I have led hands-on sessions and lectured on hyperspectral imaging at the LFD Workshop in Advanced Fluorescence Imaging and Dynamics (2021–2024), and mentored teams of undergraduates as a workshop leader for the USIBR program. I also served on UC Irvine's Associated Graduate Students board for three years, including as International Chair and Vice President of Social Affairs, working to build an inclusive graduate community.
